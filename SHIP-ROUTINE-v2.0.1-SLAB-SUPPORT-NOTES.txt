Bilal Utility v2.0.1 ship movement support update

- Movement footing now checks the actual collision-shape top against the
  player's real feet height. Half-height purpur slabs are therefore accepted
  when the player is standing at slab height.
- Ship materials recognised: purpur blocks, purpur stairs, purpur pillars,
  purpur slabs, end stone bricks, obsidian, ladders, magenta stained glass,
  magenta stained glass panes, and end rods.
- Safe floor support is limited to recognised blocks with collision shapes.
  Ladders are recognised as climbing aids, not floor. End rods are recognised
  as lights, not floor. Glass panes are accepted only where their actual
  collision shape supports the checked point.
- Existing void guard remains active; it still refuses movement when no
  matching ship surface is found ahead at the player's current height.
